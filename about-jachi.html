01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

The engineer behind the infrastructure.

I'm Jachimike Michael Onwuchekwa, an infrastructure and systems engineer in Lagos, Nigeria, working across cloud, networking, UNIX administration and DevOps automation.

THE SHORT VERSION

Technology is more than what users see.

Behind every seamless digital experience is an infrastructure that has to work.

My hands-on experience spans enterprise networking, IBM AIX and Linux administration, cloud platforms, virtualization, DevOps automation, container orchestration and database technologies. Day to day, I design, deploy, administer and troubleshoot the systems that keep services available.

I started in this field as a graduate intern in 2024 and was converted to a full-time technical role. Alongside that work, I co-founded MINDNAN, a Nigerian digital mental-health platform, which has pushed me to think about reliability, security and cost from the product side too.

Looking ahead, I'm interested in graduate research in information networking, secure cloud infrastructure, distributed systems and resilient digital-health platforms for emerging markets.
